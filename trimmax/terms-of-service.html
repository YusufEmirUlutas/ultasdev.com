Eligible accounts may receive one free analysis; its free allowance is consumed when reserved and is not restored after failure or cancellation.

Looks do not have a scheduled expiry while the account remains available. They are not money, cannot be withdrawn as cash and are not transferable between unrelated accounts. Account deletion permanently ends access to unused looks, subject to any rights required by law.
