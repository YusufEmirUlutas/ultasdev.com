Privacy policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Glute-scan photos are analysed on your iPhone and never leave it. Apple Health data stays on your iPhone. Meal photos are sent to OpenAI only to estimate nutrition and are not stored on our servers. We do not sell personal data or use advertising tracking.

1. Who handles your data

GluteMax is operated by UltasDev, the trade name of YUSUF EMİR ULUTAŞ, a sole proprietorship. “We”, “us” and “our” mean this operator.

For questions or requests, email ultasdevelopment@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, scan photos or medical records.

2. Data you provide and data the service creates

  • Account: an anonymous account identifier, or the identifier and the name or email address that Apple or Google shares when you sign in with them. Your user ID connects your records so they can be restored when you sign in on another iPhone.
  • Onboarding and profile: your answers about goals, experience, training location and equipment, focus areas, schedule and reminder time, diet preference, age, height and weight, an optional display name and referral code, and how you heard about GluteMax.
  • Training: your plan, sessions, exercises, sets, loads, repetitions and post-workout feel checks.
  • Food log: meal names, portions, times and nutrition values, whether estimated by AI, looked up by barcode or entered by you.
  • Body measurements and scan results: measurements you type in, such as weight, hips, waist and thigh, and the numerical results of glute scans, such as shape and fullness estimates. Scan photos are not included (see section 3).
  • Purchases: subscription status, product and expiry date.
  • Product interactions: limited first-party records of which features are used, to understand and improve the app. They are not shared with advertisers.
  • Operations: daily counts of AI food requests per account to apply fair-use limits, and technical request information, such as IP addresses, that servers and infrastructure providers receive.
  • On your iPhone only: glute-scan photos, small meal thumbnails, Apple Health records, cached barcode lookups, preferences and notification settings.

3. Glute scans and Apple Health

Optional glute-scan photos are analysed on your iPhone with Apple’s Vision framework. The photos stay on that iPhone, are excluded from device backups and are never sent to our servers or to an AI service. Only the numerical results are saved with your progress. They are fitness estimates, not medical assessments.

Apple Health access is optional. With your permission, GluteMax reads body weight, steps and sleep to adjust guidance on your iPhone, and writes the workouts and body weight you log. Health records read from Apple Health stay on your iPhone and are not sent to our servers, analytics or AI services. You can change access at any time in the Health app or Settings. Measurements you type into GluteMax yourself are app records and sync with your account.

4. Food estimates, OpenAI and Open Food Facts

When you ask for an estimate from a meal photo, a nutrition-label photo or a description, the resized image or your text is sent through our Supabase backend to OpenAI together with your diet preference, and OpenAI returns a nutrition estimate. Corrections you type are sent the same way. We do not save the photo on our servers; the estimate is saved in your food log, and a small thumbnail stays on your iPhone.

We ask OpenAI not to store these requests for later retrieval. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default, but it may retain request content for up to 30 days for safety and abuse monitoring under its policies, so we do not promise zero retention. OpenAI’s API data controls explain retention and exceptions.

Barcode lookups send the product barcode from your iPhone to Open Food Facts, which also receives ordinary network request information. Results are cached on your iPhone.

5. Supabase, purchases and sign-in

Supabase provides authentication, our database and backend functions. Your account and app records are stored with Supabase in the United States and protected by account-level access rules. See Supabase privacy information.

Apple processes subscription payments. RevenueCat receives purchase and subscription information associated with your GluteMax user ID to manage access, restore purchases and keep subscription status current; it also receives technical device and request information for its service. We do not receive payment-card details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

When you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, that provider handles the sign-in and shares your account identifier and, if you allow it, your name and email address with us. Google’s sign-in software may also collect device and usage information for its own service and security under Google’s privacy policy. When you delete an Apple-linked account, Apple may ask you to confirm so our server can revoke GluteMax’s access.

Workout reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. We do not sell personal data, use advertising trackers or share data with data brokers. This website is covered by our website privacy notice.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account data and app records stay associated with your account until you delete the account.
  • Deleting your account removes the account and the server records linked to it: profile, plans, workouts, food logs, measurements, scan results, product interactions, subscription status and AI usage counts. We also ask RevenueCat to delete its customer record for your user ID, and GluteMax clears its records and scan photos from that iPhone.
  • Removing the app does not delete server records. Delete your account in the app first, or contact us. An anonymous account that was never linked cannot be signed in to again after the app is removed.
  • We keep the identifiers of processed RevenueCat notifications, which contain no personal content, to prevent duplicate processing. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own transaction records under their policies.
  • Provider-side retention and infrastructure backups may outlast deletion from the active service. Contact us about a specific access or deletion request.

7. Your controls and rights

You can edit your information in the app. Use Profile → Delete account to permanently delete your account; if your subscription has ended, you can also open Account on the subscription screen. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings. Camera, photo, Health and notification permissions can be changed in iPhone Settings or the Health app, and declining them does not stop you using the rest of the app.

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete personal data, or raise an objection or restriction request where your local law provides those rights. We may need to verify the account to protect its owner. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Purpose, security and processing locations

We process your data to provide the plan, tracking, food estimates and sync you ask for, to manage your subscription, to understand which features are used, and to protect accounts and meet legal obligations. Plans, scans, food estimates and projections are fitness estimates, not medical advice or diagnoses.

Our service providers can process data outside your country, including in the United States. Their applicable contractual and legal safeguards govern those transfers. We use account access controls and encrypted connections, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Age and changes

GluteMax is intended for users aged 16 or older and is not directed at children. If you believe a child’s data was submitted, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

We will update this page and its date when our practices change.
