Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2, 2026

TL;DR: We only collect the data necessary to provide you with your workout tracking, gamification, and social features. Your workout data is synced to our secure backend (Supabase) so you can access it across devices and participate in social feeds. We never sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the GymWars app, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign up via Email, Google, or Apple, we collect your email address, display name, and optional profile picture.
  • Fitness & Workout Data: To provide core app functionality (tracking reps, sets, weights, PRs, and assigning your gamified character), we store your logged workout sessions on our secure servers.
  • Social Data: If you choose to share your workouts or interact with others on the Discover/Friends feed, that data is visible to other users as per your privacy settings.
  • Device & Usage Data: Anonymous diagnostic data and usage metrics to help us fix crashes and improve the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to enhance your experience within GymWars:

  • To authenticate your account and sync your data across your devices.
  • To analyze your workouts and assign your gamified character and stats.
  • To display your workouts to your friends and the community (if you opt-in to social features).
  • To process subscriptions via our payment provider (RevenueCat).

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to power GymWars. These services only access the data required to perform their functions:

  • Supabase: For database and secure backend infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat: For securely managing subscriptions and in-app purchases.
  • Google / Apple: For secure authentication (if you choose these login methods).

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can request to delete your account and all associated workout data at any time from within the app settings. Once requested, your data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Payment information is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). GymWars does not collect, process, or store your credit card information directly. We only receive a confirmation token to unlock premium features.
