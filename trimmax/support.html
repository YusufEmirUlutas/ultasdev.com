Support

Last updated: October 5, 2026

For a pending purchase, use Refresh balance before buying again. To free storage, delete finished results from Saved or Profile → Your photos and data. To keep a guest account, link Apple or Google.

How do I keep my guest account?

Open Profile, tap Save your looks and link Apple or Google. Linking keeps the current account’s photos, results and remaining looks. After reinstalling or on another device, sign in to that same linked account. An unlinked guest account may be lost after reinstalling or changing devices.

I paid, but my looks are missing

Keep the app on the account that made the purchase. Tap Refresh balance if the app says checkout is being verified, or use Restore on the look-purchase screen. Do not buy again while a purchase is pending. Restore can verify store transactions, but cannot recreate looks already used or recover another account’s private results. If the receipt belongs to another account, use the original-account recovery option.

Email support with the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a description of the problem. You can include the purchase date and App Store order reference; do not send payment-card details or passwords.

Are these subscriptions? Can I get a refund?

No. TrimMax look packs are one-time consumable purchases. A paid job that fails or is cancelled returns its reserved look through the server. Deleting a completed result does not refund it. For a refund of an App Store payment, use Apple’s refund service; Apple explains the process here.

Why are purchases or uploads unavailable?

The app checks server availability and account storage before new work. Open Profile → Your photos and data for actual usage and limits, and delete finished results you no longer need. Shared source photos remain until all associated results are deleted. Some availability limits affect the whole service; Refresh availability checks again. You can still refresh or restore an earlier purchase while new checkout is paused.

How do I delete photos and results?

Open Saved and use the delete control for a finished result, or go to Profile → Your photos and data. Confirm the deletion. This also removes its brief and any source/reference photos no longer used by other results. In-progress jobs must finish or be cancelled before their result can be deleted. Deleting a result does not refund used looks. Unused uploads are eligible for cleanup after 30 days.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account and review the permanent-deletion warning. Stored photos, results and access to unused looks will be lost. Deleting the app by itself does not delete your cloud account.

For Apple-linked accounts, TrimMax requests fresh Apple authorization to disconnect the account. If it cannot complete, the app offers a separate, explicit manual-disconnection option. After deletion, open iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple, select TrimMax or its developer, and choose Delete. Confirm the onscreen steps. This can also disconnect other apps using the same developer connection. Apple’s current instructions explain the process.

If you cannot reach your account, contact support. We will need to verify ownership before deleting private data.

What goes to OpenAI? Can I say no?

The AI data-sharing screen explains the photos, hair preferences, messages and relevant result context sent to OpenAI. You must explicitly agree before AI processing. You can decline and browse the catalog, or change your choice in Profile → AI data sharing. Withdrawal stops new requests; it does not recall a request already processing. See the privacy policy for retention and deletion details.

My preview or advice looks wrong

AI previews and fit scores are estimates. Try a sharp, well-lit, front-facing photo with your full hair visible and avoid hats, sunglasses and filters. You can review a reference with your barber before making changes. Do not rely on a preview for an exact haircut result or on Ask for medical advice.

Contact support
