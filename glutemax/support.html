Support

Last updated: October 8, 2026

To restore a subscription, tap Restore on the subscription screen. To cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To keep your data on a new iPhone, link Sign in with Apple or Google first.

How do I restore my subscription?

Tap Restore on the subscription screen, or open Profile → Restore purchases. Make sure your iPhone uses the Apple Account that made the purchase. If access does not return, email us with the app version, iPhone model, iOS version and the purchase date. Do not send payment-card details or passwords.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions → GluteMax. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Removing the app or deleting your GluteMax account does not cancel billing. Apple handles refunds: use Apple’s refund service; Apple explains the process here.

How do I keep my data on a new iPhone?

In Profile, link Sign in with Apple or Google, then sign in to the same account on the new iPhone. Your plan, workouts, meals, measurements and scan results come back. Glute-scan photos stay on the original iPhone and are not restored.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Delete account and confirm. If your subscription has ended, you can also open Account on the subscription screen. If you use Sign in with Apple, the app may ask you to confirm with Apple so we can revoke access. Deletion removes your data from our servers and from that iPhone. It does not cancel your subscription.

To remove GluteMax from Sign in with Apple afterwards, manage it in your Apple Account settings; Apple’s current instructions explain the steps. If you cannot reach your account, contact us; we will need to verify ownership before deleting data.

What happens to my photos?

Glute-scan photos are analysed on your iPhone and are never uploaded. Meal photos are sent to OpenAI only to estimate nutrition, and only after you allow AI meal estimates; they are not stored on our servers. You can turn AI meal estimates off in Profile. The privacy policy explains the details, including OpenAI’s retention.

How do I change permissions?

Camera, photo and notification access are in iPhone Settings → GluteMax. Apple Health access is in the Health app’s sharing settings. GluteMax keeps working if you decline any of them; for example, you can describe a meal instead of photographing it.

Can I use GluteMax offline?

You can view your saved plan and log workouts and meals offline. Sign-in, purchase verification, AI food estimates and new barcode lookups need an internet connection. Saved changes sync when you are back online.

A food estimate looks wrong

AI estimates can be off. Tap any value to adjust it, or use Fix result to describe what was wrong. For better estimates, photograph the whole plate from above in good light, or scan the barcode of packaged food.

Contact support

For questions or requests, email ultasdevelopment@gmail.com. Include the app version, iPhone model and a description of what happened. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, scan photos or medical records.
