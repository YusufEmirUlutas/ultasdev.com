Privacy policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

TrimMax stores your selected photos and results privately on our backend. OpenAI receives data for AI features only after you explicitly agree in the app. You can withdraw that permission and delete saved results or your account.

1. Who handles your data

TrimMax is operated by UltasDev, the trade name of YUSUF EMİR ULUTAŞ, a sole proprietorship. “We”, “us” and “our” mean this operator.

For questions or requests, email ultasdevelopment@gmail.com. Please do not send payment-card details, passwords or photos unless support specifically needs them and you choose to share them.

2. Data you provide and data the service creates

  • Account: a guest account identifier, or the account identifier and information supplied when you link Apple or Google, such as an email address. Your server account connects your photos, results and remaining looks.
  • Photos: the image you choose or capture, the crop you approve, and any reference image you choose for a hairstyle preview. The app does not upload your entire photo library.
  • Hair preferences: answers about your hair, current length, maintenance, desired style and age range, when supplied. An optional name is kept in the local profile and is not included in our AI requests.
  • Results: generated preview images, analysis reports, face-shape and hair-texture estimates, recommendations, barber briefs, routines and job status.
  • Chat: messages and relevant conversation context you send to Ask, together with hair preferences and result context needed to answer.
  • Operations: request and job identifiers, timestamps, consent records, credit debits and refunds, purchase-verification records, error and security information. Servers and infrastructure providers receive technical request information such as IP addresses.
  • On your device: preferences, saved catalog references, onboarding progress and recovery markers. A temporary local source-photo copy can support recovery of an interrupted onboarding flow.

3. Supabase, OpenAI and your choice

Supabase provides authentication, our database, backend functions and private photo storage. Account ownership checks and short-lived signed links control access to private photos and results. We use this data to provide and recover the actions you request. See Supabase privacy information.

OpenAI processes the selected photo or reference photo, hair preferences and requested style for analysis and previews. For Ask, it processes your message, relevant conversation history and hair/result context. The in-app AI data-sharing screen explains this before you agree. We do not send your Apple or Google sign-in credentials or payment details to OpenAI.

You may decline and browse the catalog without AI processing. You can withdraw permission in Profile → AI data sharing. This stops new AI requests and requests cancellation of pending work; it does not recall data already sent to a processor or delete existing results. You can delete results separately.

AI requests can use background processing, which temporarily stores provider response state so the job can finish and be retrieved. OpenAI may also retain data for safety and abuse monitoring under its applicable policies. We do not promise zero retention. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train models by default; its API data controls explain retention and exceptions.

4. Purchases and other services

Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat verifies consumable look purchases and associates transaction data with your TrimMax account identifier so our server can grant credits once. We do not receive payment-card details. RevenueCat also receives technical device and request information used for its service; see its privacy policy. These are one-time consumable purchases, not subscriptions.

Apple or Google handles sign-in when you choose to link an account. Apple may be asked for fresh authorization when deleting an Apple-linked account so the server can revoke its connection. Those fresh credentials are used for that operation and are not saved in app preferences.

When enabled, Apple App Attest checks app integrity using a device-backed key, server challenges and assertions. These checks help protect the service from automated abuse; they are not advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking.

We do not sell personal data or use advertising tracking. Sharing a barber brief or opening an external link sends information to the app or website you choose. This website is covered by our website privacy notice.

5. How long we keep data

  • Photos and saved results remain associated with your account until you delete the associated results or delete the account. A source or reference photo reused by several jobs remains until all jobs that reference it have been deleted.
  • Uploads that are not associated with a job are eligible for automatic cleanup after 30 days.
  • Deleting a result removes its report, generated photo and any source/reference photos no longer needed by another result. This does not delete or refund the credit transaction that paid for the job.
  • Account deletion removes your account and its photos and results, and ends access to its remaining looks. We retain minimum transaction identifiers, product and transaction status, webhook event identifiers and account-deletion or Apple-revocation proof needed to prevent fraud, duplicate purchase grants and unauthorized account reuse, or meet legal obligations. These records can still be personal data even after account contact details and photos are removed. Operational AI-budget metadata is detached from the account and retained for 90 days. Apple and RevenueCat keep their own transaction records under their policies.
  • Provider-side retention and infrastructure backup retention may outlast deletion from the active TrimMax service. Contact us about a specific access or deletion request.

6. Your controls and rights

Use Saved or Profile → Your photos and data to delete individual finished results. Use Profile → Delete account to permanently delete the account. Removing the app alone does not delete server-held photos or results. Camera and photo permissions can be managed in iPhone Settings.

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete personal data, or raise an objection or restriction request where your local law provides those rights. We may need to verify the account to protect its owner. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of processing already completed.

7. Purpose, security and processing locations

We process account, photo, result and purchase data to provide the service you request, use your permission for optional AI sharing, and use necessary operational information to protect accounts, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. AI estimates describe hairstyle suitability, not identity or attractiveness, and are not medical diagnoses.

Our service providers can process data outside your country. Their applicable contractual and legal safeguards govern those transfers. We use private storage and account access controls, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children and changes

TrimMax is not directed at children under 13. Do not submit a child’s personal data without the legally required permission. If you believe a child’s data was submitted improperly, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

We will update this page and its date when practices change. Material changes to AI sharing require the applicable in-app consent.
