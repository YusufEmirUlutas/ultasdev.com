Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

TL;DR: ReadMax keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your reading sessions, book positions, imported books, notes and settings never leave your device. The one exception is subscriptions: Apple takes the payment, and RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID and the purchase details it needs to confirm that your subscription is active. We never see your card details, and we never sell or share your data.

1. Who We Are

ReadMax: Speed Reading Trainer (“ReadMax” or “the app”) is made by UltasDev. UltasDev is a trademark of YUSUF EMİR ULUTAŞ, a sole proprietorship. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to UltasDev.

This policy explains what ReadMax stores, what (if anything) leaves your device, and how you can delete it. If anything here is unclear, email us at ultasdevelopment@gmail.com.

2. What ReadMax Does

ReadMax measures your reading speed (words per minute) and comprehension, gives you short paced practice each day, and lets you read at your trained pace. It ships with a library of public-domain classics, lets you import your own EPUB or TXT books, shows your reading progress on a calendar, and can send you an optional daily reminder.

3. Data That Stays on Your Device

ReadMax stores the following on your device only. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Reading test results: your reading speed and comprehension scores.
  • Reading sessions: when you read or trained, for how long, and at what pace. This is what powers your progress calendar.
  • Book positions: where you left off in each book.
  • Imported books: the EPUB or TXT files you add to your library (see section 6).
  • Notes: any reading notes you write in the app.
  • Settings: your reader preferences, pace, and whether the daily reminder is on.

ReadMax has no account system. You never sign in, and there is no cloud backup or sync run by us. Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, recover it or hand it over to anyone.

If you use Apple’s own device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), the app’s data may be included in that backup like any other app’s. That is controlled by your Apple settings, not by ReadMax.

4. Subscriptions: The One Thing That Leaves Your Device

ReadMax is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through the App Store. Two parties are involved in processing it:

Apple

Apple handles the payment itself. Your card or payment details go to Apple, never to ReadMax or to us. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to everything you do in the App Store.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and confirm that your purchase is valid. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID that is generated on your device. It is a random identifier; it is not your name, email or Apple ID.
  • Purchase and subscription events and the receipt data Apple provides, so that the subscription can be verified and restored on your device.
  • Device and app metadata needed to validate purchases, such as the app version and device type.

That is all. Your reading data, your books and your notes are never sent to RevenueCat.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use any analytics SDK. We do not know how you use the app.
  • We do not show advertising and do not include any advertising SDK.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with any third party, other than the subscription processing by Apple and RevenueCat described in section 4.

6. Imported Books

When you import an EPUB or TXT file, ReadMax copies it into the app’s private storage on your device so it can be opened later. The file is never uploaded anywhere. It is deleted when you delete the book from your library, and it is deleted along with everything else when you delete the app.

7. Daily Reminder

The daily reminder is a local notification. It is scheduled on your device and only if you turn it on in Settings. There is no push notification server behind it, and nothing about your reminder leaves your device. You can switch it off in the app at any time.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • In the app: Settings › Erase all data deletes everything ReadMax stores: sessions, test results, positions, imported books, notes and settings.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data as well.
  • RevenueCat records: the subscription records tied to your anonymous ID can be deleted on request. Email us at ultasdevelopment@gmail.com and we will arrange it.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal data, such as the right to access or delete it. Since ReadMax keeps its data on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app. For anything involving RevenueCat, email us.

9. Children

ReadMax is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Since the app collects no personal data beyond the anonymous subscription ID, there is nothing for us to hold; but if you believe a child has subscribed and want the RevenueCat record removed, email us.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we change how ReadMax handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app.
