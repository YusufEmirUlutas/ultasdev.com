ReadMax: Speed Reading Trainer

Read faster. Understand more. Short daily lessons push your pace, and your speed only counts when you understood what you read.

Download on theApp Store

Available for iPhone.

What ReadMax Does

  • 01
    Train

    A path of short daily lessons, built like a game. Each one starts with a quick tip, then the phrase pacer pushes you past the pace you hold. Six units take you from Find your pace to Remember more, and each ends with a speed check that opens the next. Streaks, XP, a daily goal and six levels keep you going, and Max, the ReadMax mascot, runs beside you.

  • 02
    Prove it

    Four questions follow every passage. Your real speed is the fastest pace at which you got at least 3 of 4 right, so it only counts what you understood, and it never drops. A short reading test shows where you start next to the average adult, about 240 words a minute (Brysbaert, 2019).

  • 03
    Read

    Twelve public-domain classics are built in, and you can import your own EPUB or text files or paste an article. Read with a pacer that sweeps under the lines, or one word at a time in the focus reader. Each chapter you read through earns XP.

  • 04
    Progress

    See your real speed and its last two weeks, your level, your streak and your achievements. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no sync, no tracking.
