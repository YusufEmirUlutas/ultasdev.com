Support

Answers to common questions about ReadMax: Speed Reading Trainer, and a way to reach us if yours is not here.

Quick fixes: lost your subscription after reinstalling? Settings › Restore purchases. Want to cancel? That happens in your App Store subscription settings, not in the app. Want to start over? Settings › Erase all data. Settings opens from the button at the top right of the Progress tab.
How do I restore my purchase?

Open ReadMax, go to the Progress tab, tap the settings button at the top right, then Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore on the subscription screen. Make sure your device is signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so cancelling happens in your App Store settings rather than inside ReadMax. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose ReadMax and tap Cancel Subscription. In ReadMax, Settings › Manage subscription opens Apple’s subscription settings too.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of your current period to avoid the next charge. You keep access until that period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

What is my real speed, and why didn’t it go up?

Your real speed is the fastest pace at which you got at least 3 of the 4 questions after a passage right, in a lesson, a speed check or the reading test, the first time you read that passage. A read with fewer than 3 right doesn’t count, and your next lesson eases off a little. A passage you have read before doesn’t count either, because you have already seen its questions.

Your real speed itself never goes down. It rises when you understand a new passage read faster than your best.

How do I open the next unit?

Each unit ends with a speed check: one passage read at your own pace, with no pacer, then four questions. Get at least 3 right and the next unit opens. If you don’t, try again whenever you like, with a different passage.

How do I import my own book?

Go to the Read tab and tap + at the top right. Choose Import a file and pick an EPUB or text file (up to 20 MB), or choose Paste text. Books with DRM, like most shop purchases, can’t be opened. ReadMax copies what you add into the app on your device; it is never uploaded anywhere.

To remove a book you added, tap it in Read, then tap the ··· button at the top right and choose Delete book.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

ReadMax sets the reminder for the time you pick at “When will you train?” in the introduction, if you allow notifications, and keeps it while your subscription is active. To change the time or switch it off, go to Settings › Reminder. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device, so it only works while notifications for ReadMax are allowed in your iPhone’s Settings.

Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

Everything ReadMax stores, including your lessons and results, your place in each book, the books you added, your bookmarks and your settings, lives only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, and we cannot see any of it.

To delete it all, go to Settings › Erase all data. Deleting the app from your device removes it too. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Your progress is stored on your iPhone, not on a server of ours. If you set up a new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, or transfer directly from the old one, ReadMax’s data comes along like any other app’s. If you delete ReadMax, its progress is deleted with it.

Your subscription belongs to your Apple Account: on a new iPhone, use Restore purchases.

Can I retake the reading test?

Yes. Go to Settings › Retake the reading test. A retake reads a passage you haven’t read yet, so it can count toward your real speed. Once you have read every passage, a retake is practice only.

Do I need an account?

No. ReadMax has no sign-in. Your subscription is tied to your Apple Account, and everything else stays on your device.
