ReadMax: Speed Reading Trainer

Know how fast you really read, train it a few minutes a day, and keep the comprehension while you do.

Download on theApp Store

Available for iPhone.

What ReadMax Does

  • 01
    Measure

    A short reading test gives you your real reading speed in words per minute and a comprehension score to go with it. Retake it whenever you want to see where you stand.

  • 02
    Train

    A few minutes of paced practice each day. Your progress calendar shows the days you read, and an optional daily reminder helps the habit stick.

  • 03
    Read

    Read public-domain classics from the built-in library, or import your own EPUB and TXT books, all at your trained pace. Everything stays on your device: no account, no sync, no tracking.
