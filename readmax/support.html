Support

Answers to the questions we hear most about ReadMax: Speed Reading Trainer, and a way to reach us if yours is not here.

Quick fixes: lost your subscription after reinstalling? Settings › Restore purchases. Want to cancel? That happens in your App Store subscription settings, not in the app. Want to start over? Settings › Erase all data.
How do I restore my purchase?

Open ReadMax, go to Settings › Restore purchases. You can also tap Restore on the subscription screen. Make sure your device is signed in to the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so cancelling happens in your App Store settings rather than inside ReadMax. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose ReadMax and tap Cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of your current period to avoid the next charge. You keep access until that period ends. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us.

How do I import my own book?

Go to Library › Add › Import a file and pick an EPUB or TXT file. ReadMax copies it into the app on your device; it is never uploaded anywhere. To remove it later, delete the book from your library.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off?

Go to Settings › Reminder. The reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device, so it only works while notifications for ReadMax are allowed in your iPhone’s Settings. Switch it off in the same place whenever you like.

Where is my data stored, and how do I delete it?

Everything ReadMax stores, including your reading sessions, book positions, imported books, notes and settings, lives only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, and we cannot see any of it.

To delete it all, go to Settings › Erase all data. Deleting the app from your device removes it too. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Can I retake the reading test?

Yes. Go to Settings › Retake the reading test to measure your reading speed and comprehension again.

Do I need an account?

No. ReadMax has no sign-in. Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID, and everything else stays on your device.
