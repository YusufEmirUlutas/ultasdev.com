Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

TL;DR: ReadMax keeps your reading on your iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud sync, no analytics SDK, no advertising and no tracking. Your lessons, test results, reading positions, the books you add, your bookmarks and your settings never leave your device. The one exception is subscriptions: Apple takes the payment, and RevenueCat receives a random ID made on your device and the purchase details it needs to confirm that your subscription is active. We never see your card details, and we never sell or share your data.

1. Who We Are

ReadMax: Speed Reading Trainer (“ReadMax” or “the app”) is made by UltasDev. UltasDev is a trademark of YUSUF EMİR ULUTAŞ, a sole proprietorship. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to UltasDev.

This policy explains what ReadMax stores, what (if anything) leaves your device, and how you can delete it. If anything here is unclear, email us at ultasdevelopment@gmail.com.

2. What ReadMax Does

ReadMax is a speed-reading trainer. A path of short daily lessons pushes your pace with a pacer and asks four questions after every passage, and your real speed (words per minute) counts only when you get at least 3 of 4 right. ReadMax ships with a library of public-domain classics, lets you import your own EPUB or text files or paste text, keeps your streak, XP, level and achievements, and can send you a daily reminder.

3. Data That Stays on Your Device

ReadMax stores the following on your device only. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Reading test results: the pace you read the test at and how many of its questions you got right.
  • Lessons, sprints and speed checks: which passage you read, the pace you read it at, how many questions you got right and how long it took. Your real speed, XP, streak, daily goal, level and achievements are all counted from these records, on your device.
  • Your reading in books: where you left off in each book, the chapters you finished, how long you read, and your bookmarks with any line you add to one.
  • Books you add: the EPUB or text files you import and the text you paste (see section 6).
  • Your answers in the introduction: such as how many books you read last year, the topics you like, your books goal and when you plan to train. They shape your plan, the practice passages you get and the order of the library.
  • Your name, if you type one in Settings.
  • Settings: the app’s appearance, your reader preferences and themes, your daily goal and your reminder time.

ReadMax has no account system. You never sign in, and there is no cloud backup or sync run by us. Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot see it, recover it or hand it over to anyone.

If you use Apple’s own device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), the app’s data may be included in that backup like any other app’s. That is controlled by your Apple settings, not by ReadMax.

4. Subscriptions: The One Thing That Leaves Your Device

ReadMax is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through the App Store. Two parties are involved in processing it:

Apple

Apple handles the payment itself. Your card or payment details go to Apple, never to ReadMax or to us. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to everything you do in the App Store.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and confirm that your purchase is valid. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID that is generated on your device. It is a random identifier; it is not your name, email or Apple Account. A new one is made if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • Purchase and subscription events and the transaction data Apple provides, so that the subscription can be verified, renewed and restored on your device.
  • Technical details sent with each request: the device model, the iOS and app versions, your language and App Store country, and the identifier for vendor (a code iOS gives one developer’s apps on one device; it is not the advertising identifier). Like any server, RevenueCat also sees the IP address a request comes from.

RevenueCat’s dashboard shows us subscription figures, such as how many people subscribed, renewed or cancelled. That is purchase data only. Your lessons, results, books, bookmarks and settings are never sent to RevenueCat or to us. RevenueCat’s own privacy policy describes how it handles this data.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use any analytics SDK. We do not see your lessons, your results or what you read.
  • We do not show advertising, do not include any advertising SDK, and never ask for or use your device’s advertising identifier.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with any third party, other than the subscription processing by Apple and RevenueCat described in section 4.

Two things you start yourself also use the internet. The Share button on your streak hands a line of text to your iPhone’s share sheet, and it goes wherever you choose to send it. The Privacy policy and Terms of use links open this website, and Support opens your own mail app; our website privacy notice covers both.

6. Imported Books

When you import an EPUB or text file, or paste text, ReadMax copies it into the app’s private storage on your device so it can be opened later. It is never uploaded anywhere. It is deleted when you delete the book from your library, and along with everything else when you erase all data or delete the app.

7. Daily Reminder

ReadMax can remind you once a day to train. When you answer “When will you train?” in the introduction, ReadMax asks your iPhone for permission to send notifications and, only if you allow it, schedules the reminder for that time while your subscription is active. You can change the time or switch it off in Settings › Reminder, or turn off ReadMax’s notifications in your iPhone’s Settings app.

The reminder is a local notification: it is scheduled on your device, there is no push notification server behind it, and nothing about it leaves your device.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • In the app: Settings › Erase all data deletes everything ReadMax stores on your device: your test results, every lesson and practice session, the books you added, your place in each book, your bookmarks, your answers in the introduction and your settings. It also cancels the daily reminder. Your subscription is not affected. Settings opens from the button at the top right of the Progress tab.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data as well.
  • RevenueCat records: the subscription records tied to your anonymous ID can be deleted on request. Email us at ultasdevelopment@gmail.com and we will arrange it.

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal data, such as the right to access or delete it. Since ReadMax keeps its data on your device, you can exercise most of these directly in the app. For anything involving RevenueCat, email us.

9. Children

ReadMax is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Since the app collects no personal data beyond the anonymous subscription ID, there is nothing for us to hold; but if you believe a child has subscribed and want the RevenueCat record removed, email us.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we change how ReadMax handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the app.
